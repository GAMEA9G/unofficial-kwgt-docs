Containers

StackLayerModule (Row)

PropertyMeaning
config_stackingHORIZONTAL_CENTER and HORIZONTAL_TOP verified. VERTICAL_CENTER unconfirmed.
config_marginGap. Negative values such as -4.4 overlap items on purpose.
position_*Anchor and padding work on stacks.

OverlapLayerModule (Stack)

PropertyMeaning
fx_maskCLIP_ALL clips all children, CLIP_NEXT only the next layer.
config_fx_radiusRounds the group, e.g. 10.0.
config_rotate_modeRotates the whole container: CLOCK_HOUR_SMOOTH, CLOCK_MINUTE, CLOCK_SECOND or MANUAL. See Analog clock.
config_rotate_offsetExtra rotation in degrees (90.0 seen).
config_visibleNeeds toggle 10. Returns ALWAYS, NEVER or REMOVE (I read REMOVE as taking it out of the layout; see Calendar widget).
internal_eventsAny module accepts taps.

KomponentModule

A reusable block with its own globals_list (private, never leaks to root; indexes need not start at 1), internal_title, internal_description and optional visibility formula (stored with toggle 0 it is inactive). Real presets often wrap the whole widget in one. How a container sizes itself from children is unconfirmed.
