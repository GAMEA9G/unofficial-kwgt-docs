Events and taps

FieldMeaning
typeSINGLE_TAP verified. DOUBLE_TAP, LONG_TAP unconfirmed. May be omitted at root.
actionMUSIC, LAUNCH_APP, OPEN_LINK, KUSTOM_ACTION and DISABLED are all seen in real presets.
music_actionNEXT, PREVIOUS, OPEN_APP. Play/pause has none.
intentFor LAUNCH_APP: an Android intent such as intent:#Intent;action=android.intent.action.MAIN;category=android.intent.category.LAUNCHER;component=com.google.android.deskclock/com.android.deskclock.DeskClock;S.org.kustom.intent.label=Clock;end. For OPEN_LINK use url instead (it can be a formula). A transparent shape works as an invisible tap area.
