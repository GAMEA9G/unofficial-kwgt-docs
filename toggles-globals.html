The toggle trap. A formula or global is ignored unless its toggle selects it.
"internal_toggles": { "paint_color": 100, "shape_width": 10 },
"internal_globals": { "paint_color": "accent" },

Global types seen

COLOR, NUMBER, TEXT, FONT, plus LIST (a pick-one setting with entries) and BITMAP (an image path). Names may contain a slash, like icon/clear. Duplicate or skipped index numbers still load. See Weather icons.

Built-in globals: w and h are the root width and height. With toggle 100 the plain value is optional; many presets keep it as fallback.
