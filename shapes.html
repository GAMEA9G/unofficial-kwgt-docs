Shapes

ShapeModule draws backgrounds, dots and progress bars.

PropertyMeaning
shape_typeRECT, CIRCLE, ARC (verified). OVAL, EXAGON, PATH unconfirmed. Keep the spelling EXAGON.
shape_width / _heightPixels (floats like 546.0). Can be driven by a global (toggle 100) or formula (toggle 10).
shape_cornersCorner radius. 360 = fully rounded pill.
shape_angleArc sweep in degrees (90.0 for wave crests).
shape_rotate_mode"DEG180" flips an arc into a trough.
paint_strokeStroke width (3.0 seen on the analog clock border).
paint_color#AARRGGBB. #00FFFFFF is transparent.
