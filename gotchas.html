Gotchas and install

  • First item = bottom layer.
  • Toggles 0/10/100 decide the source.
  • globals_list is an object.
  • Bitmaps come from formulas.
  • Visibility returns ALWAYS or NEVER.
  • Play/pause has no music_action; root events may omit type.
  • Keep odd spellings (EXAGON) and exact decimals (-4.4).
  • Formulas appear with or without $...$.
  • Keep a static fallback when you can.

Install

  1. Zip preset.json (+ thumbnails, folders) into a .kwgt, preset.json at the root.
  2. Open it from KWGT load, or put it in Kustom > widgets.
  3. Add a KWGT widget, pick the preset, tap Save.
