Positioning

Each module clings to an anchor of its parent, padding pushes inward from that edge (applied first), then offset nudges it.

PropertyMeaning
position_anchorTOPLEFT (default), TOPRIGHT, CENTERLEFT, CENTERRIGHT, BOTTOM, BOTTOMLEFT, BOTTOMRIGHT, CENTER
position_padding_left/_top/_right/_bottomDistance inward from the anchor edge.
position_offset_x / _yExtra move. With TOPLEFT it is plain x and y.
