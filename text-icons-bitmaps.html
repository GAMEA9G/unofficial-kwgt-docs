Text, icons, images

TextModule / CurvedTextModule

PropertyMeaning
text_expressionText or formula. BBcode like [b] works. Bare words are strings.
text_familyFont path (kfile://...).
text_sizeFloat, e.g. 32.0.
text_alignCENTER verified (LEFT and RIGHT expected).
text_size_type, text_width, text_lines"FIXED_WIDTH" keeps text inside a given width (e.g. 1.0 or 2.0 lines).
paint_colorColor, often bound to a global.
fx_shadow*Text shares the shape shadow properties.

FontIconModule

PropertyMeaning
icon_iconMaterial name: skip_next, skip_previous, music_note, headset_mic. Dynamic icons use internal_formulas with toggle 10 and keep a static fallback.
icon_sizePixels, e.g. 42.0.
icon_setOptional icon font path. Material names resolve without it.

BitmapModule

PropertyMeaning
bitmap_bitmapSet only through internal_formulas (toggle 10), e.g. $mi(cover)$ or $ni(mi(package), icon)$. No static property.
bitmap_width / _heightPixels.
bitmap_filter"COLORIZE" tints the image.
bitmap_scale_mode"CENTER_FIT" fits the image inside its box, centered (seen in Weather Now Icons).
bitmap_filter_colorTint color, often a global.
