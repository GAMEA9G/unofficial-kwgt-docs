Formulas

Wrap in $...$ (stored ones may be bare). Strings are bare words. Use =, !=, | for or.

FunctionGivesExample

Multi-line scripts

Use \n for sequential logic. Turning it into a space causes a formula error.

"position_padding_left": "$lv(\"w\",gv(pbwidth)*(mi(percent)/100))$\n$if(lv(w)<=0, \"0\", lv(w)-8)$"
